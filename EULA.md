## 3. What the plugin sends and stores

- **Network:** the plugin connects only to `xkcd.com` and `imgs.xkcd.com`, to fetch comic information and images. Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to xkcd's servers, and they identify the plugin as `xkcdviewer` with its version number. xkcd's own policies apply to those servers.
- **Browser:** pressing a comic opens that comic's page on xkcd.com in your default browser.
- **On your computer:** the plugin saves three comic numbers in its Logi plugin settings: the last comic you viewed, the newest comic it has told you about, and the newest comic it knows of. Comic images are kept in memory only, and are gone when the plugin stops.
- **Nothing else:** the plugin has no analytics, no tracking and no account. The author receives no data about you or your use of the plugin.
